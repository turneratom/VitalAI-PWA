Smart glasses put a camera on your nose. Halo uses the hat — higher, wider, and invisible to everyone else.

See

Forward POV plus peripheral and rear cameras under the brim. Scene understanding, OCR, people approaching from behind.

👂

Hear

Beamforming mic array in the sweatband isolates your voice. Knows who's speaking and from which direction.

💬

Interface

Bone conduction replies, brim LED in your periphery, haptic taps. No earbuds. No screen. Just conversation.
