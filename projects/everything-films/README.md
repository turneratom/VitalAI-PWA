## Outreach engine

`/outreach/` covers:

1. **Wedding planners** — partner emails to refer couples
2. **Funeral parlors** — partner emails for life movies
3. **Wedding announcements** — journey email when announced
4. **Birth announcements** — after a 14-day cooling window
5. **Obituaries** — soft journey email after a 7-day cooling window

CSV import: `data/announcements.sample.csv`

Sample partner emails use `.example` addresses — swap real lists before sending.
