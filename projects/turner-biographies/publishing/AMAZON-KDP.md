## Amazon blurb (paste into KDP description)

He arrived in America with almost nothing—and wrote the words that made independence speakable.

From a cordwainer’s shop in Thetford to the printing presses of Philadelphia, from the frozen retreats of the Continental Army to a prison cell in revolutionary France, Thomas Paine lived every consequence of the ideas he unleashed. His pamphlets—*Common Sense*, *The American Crisis*, *Rights of Man*—did not merely comment on history. They moved it.

In *Firebrand*, Brad Turner tells Paine’s story as a lived novel: restless, wounded, brilliant, and unwilling to stay quiet. You stand in the ink smell of the shop. You cross the Atlantic with Franklin’s letter against your chest. You hear “these are the times that try men’s souls” before Trenton. You watch a republic he helped midwife turn its face away.

This is Book One of *The Greatest Humans*—immersive biographies of the lives that shaped the world, written as stories and built for the screen.

## KDP upload checklist

1. Go to [kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com) → **Create** → Kindle eBook (then add Paperback)
2. Language: English  
3. Author: Brad Turner  
4. Series: The Greatest Humans, Book 1  
5. Description: paste blurb above (HTML bold/italic OK in KDP editor)  
6. Publishing rights: **I own the copyright**  
7. Keywords + categories as above  
8. Manuscript: upload `Firebrand-Brad-Turner.epub` (or `.docx`)  
9. Cover: upload `Firebrand-ebook-cover-1600x2560.jpg`  
10. Price → Publish  
11. For paperback: trim **6 × 9 in**, paper **white**, upload wrap cover, set page count ~210 if prompted  

Paperback spine was calculated for **210 pages, white paper**. After KDP shows the exact page count from your file, regenerate the wrap if the spine width warning appears.
