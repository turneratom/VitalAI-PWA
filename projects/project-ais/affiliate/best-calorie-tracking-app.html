FAQ

What is the most accurate calorie tracking app?

Cronometer is the most accurate for micronutrients because it uses verified database entries. MyFitnessPal has the largest crowd-sourced database, which is convenient but occasionally inconsistent. Always cross-check packaged foods against the label.

Is there a completely free calorie tracker?

Yes. MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, and Lose It! all offer usable free tiers. Premium unlocks barcode scan history, macros by meal, and ad removal on some apps.

Can AI count calories from a photo?

AI photo estimation is improving but still approximate. Apps like Lose It! Snap It and VitalAI camera chat give quick estimates; use a dedicated tracker for daily totals and verify with a food scale when precision matters.

Which app is best for athletes?

Cronometer for detailed macro and micronutrient splits, or MyFitnessPal for ease and wearable sync. Pair with VitalAI's Protein Calculator to set intake by body weight and goal.
