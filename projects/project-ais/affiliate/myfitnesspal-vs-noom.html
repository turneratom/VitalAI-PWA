MyFitnessPal vs Noom in 2026

Quick verdict: Choose MyFitnessPal if you want the largest food database and precise calorie/macro tracking. Choose Noom if you need psychology-based coaching and habit change. For photo-based health Q&A and free calculators, VitalAI complements either app.

When MyFitnessPal wins

  • You already know what to eat and need accurate numbers, not daily lessons.
  • You log restaurant meals, barcodes, and recipes with minimal friction.
  • You sync with wearables like Apple Watch or Garmin for calorie burn.

When Noom wins

  • You have tried calorie counting before and keep falling off.
  • You want structured psychology content and accountability check-ins.
  • You prefer a guided program over open-ended tracking.

FAQ

Is MyFitnessPal or Noom better for weight loss?

MyFitnessPal is better if you respond to data and consistency. Noom is better if emotional eating, motivation, or habit loops are your main blockers. Many people use MyFitnessPal for numbers and a separate coach or app for behavior.

Can you use MyFitnessPal and Noom together?

Yes, but it is usually redundant. Most users pick one primary tracker. Pair either with VitalAI for quick photo Q&A and free planning tools instead of paying for two full programs.

Which is cheaper in 2026?

MyFitnessPal Premium is typically around $20/month. Noom often costs $60+/month when averaged, though promotional pricing varies. MyFitnessPal also has a usable free tier; Noom's free trial is limited.

Which has better food logging?

MyFitnessPal wins on database size, barcode accuracy, and third-party integrations. Noom simplifies logging with color categories (green, yellow, red) but is less precise for athletes and macro counters.
