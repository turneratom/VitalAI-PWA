Water Intake FAQ

How much water should I drink per day?

A common rule is 30–35 ml per kg body weight, plus extra for exercise and heat. Most adults need 2–3 liters total.

Does coffee count toward water intake?

Yes — caffeinated drinks contribute to hydration, though water remains the best baseline.

Signs of dehydration?

Dark urine, headache, fatigue, and dizziness. Aim for pale yellow urine as a simple check.
