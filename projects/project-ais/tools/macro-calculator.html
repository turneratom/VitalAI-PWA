Macro Calculator FAQ

What are macros?

Macronutrients are protein, carbohydrates, and fat — the three main sources of calories in food.

How much protein should I eat?

Most active adults benefit from 1.6–2.2 g per kg of body weight. Our high-protein preset targets the upper range.

Do I need to hit macros exactly?

No — aim within ±10 g per macro. Consistency over weeks matters more than daily perfection.
