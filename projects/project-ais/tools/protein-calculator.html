Protein Calculator FAQ

How much protein do I need to build muscle?

Research suggests 1.6–2.2 g per kg body weight for muscle gain, with higher end for lean bulking and heavy training.

Can you eat too much protein?

Healthy kidneys generally handle high protein. Very high intakes (>3 g/kg) rarely add benefit for most people.

Best protein sources?

Chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, legumes, and whey isolate. Spread intake across 3–4 meals for best absorption.
