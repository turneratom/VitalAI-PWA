The route

Medina, mountains, dunes.

Hosts, transfers, and camps are arranged. You keep the hours that don’t need a plan.

Day 01

Arrive Marrakech

Private transfer into the medina. Settle into a quiet riad. Rooftop tea at dusk; nothing scheduled after dark.

Day 02

Medina at walking pace

Morning with a local host through souks and side streets timed to miss the crush. Long lunch in a courtyard. Afternoon free for hammam or shade.

Day 03

Gardens & craft

A half-day shaped around what you want: Majorelle light, a private atelier visit, or a food walk away from the square. Evening open.

Day 04

Into the High Atlas

Drive into the mountains. Village lunch, a short walk for air and view, overnight in a lodge above the valley.

Day 05

Toward the dunes

Cross the foothills toward the desert. Camel or 4×4 into camp before sunset. Dinner under the stars; no Wi‑Fi theater — just the night.

Day 06

Desert morning → return

Sunrise on the dunes. Slow breakfast, then the road back toward Marrakech with stops for lunch and light. Riad again by evening.

Day 07

Slow exit

Late checkout where possible. One last reserved lunch or hammam hour, then private transfer to the airport — timed without drama.
