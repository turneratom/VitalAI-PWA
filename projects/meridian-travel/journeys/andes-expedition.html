The route

Two countries. One high arc.

Flights, border timing, and altitude days are arranged. You keep the hours that don’t need a plan.

Day 01

Arrive Lima

Private transfer from the airport. Settle into a Miraflores or Barranco stay. Soft evening — ocean air, a reserved dinner, early night before the climb inland.

Day 02

Lima at walking pace

Host morning through Barranco or the historic center. Long lunch by the Pacific. Afternoon free for a gallery, a market, or rest before the Andes.

Day 03

Fly to Cusco · acclimatize

Morning flight into the highlands. Check into a quiet stay near the Plaza. Coca tea, a short walk if you feel ready — nothing forced on day one at altitude.

Day 04

Cusco lanes & plazas

Gentle host morning through San Blas and the cathedral quarter. Long lunch. Afternoon free to nap, wander, or sit with the light on the stones.

Day 05

Sacred Valley

Private drive down to Pisac or Chinchero — markets, terraces, and a valley lunch that doesn’t rush. Overnight in the Sacred Valley at a lower, kinder altitude.

Day 06

Valley ruins & Ollantaytambo

Morning at a quieter site timed to miss the crush. Afternoon in Ollantaytambo’s living Inca streets. Early dinner before the next day’s train.

Day 07

Machu Picchu

Train into Aguas Calientes. Guided visit of Machu Picchu with timed entry and room to pause. Overnight below the citadel — or return to the valley if you prefer.

Day 08

Second light or return to Cusco

Optional dawn return to the site if tickets allow — or a slow train back to Cusco with the afternoon free. Your call by the night before.

Day 09

Open day in Cusco

Left blank on purpose — a weaving workshop, Sacsayhuamán if you have the legs, shopping, or nothing at all in a courtyard café.

Day 10

Overland to Lake Titicaca

Scenic drive or rail toward Puno. Arrive to lake light and a waterfront stay. Soft evening — altitude again, so dinner stays early.

Day 11

Titicaca islands

Private boat day on the lake — Uros and Taquile or a quieter island route with a home lunch. Return by late afternoon with the water still silver.

Day 12

Cross into Bolivia

Border formalities handled. Continue to Copacabana or onward to La Paz. Settle into a high-city stay with views over the bowl of lights.

Day 13

La Paz at walking pace

Host morning through the historic center and a market that fits your brief. Cable car if you want the long view. Afternoon free for rest or a quiet café.

Day 14

South to the salt flats

Flight or private transfer toward Uyuni. Check into a desert or salt-edge stay. Briefing for the flats — early night under a hard, clear sky.

Day 15

Salar de Uyuni

Full day on the salt with a private driver-guide — Isla Incahuasi, endless horizon, and lunch out on the white. Light and silence do most of the talking.

Day 16

Lagunas or open desert day

South toward the colored lagoons and volcano rim if the season and roads allow — or a second, slower pass on the salar. We hold both options until you choose.

Day 17

Return to La Paz

Morning transfer or flight north. One last reserved dinner in the city. Pack without haste; the hard miles are behind you.

Day 18

Departure

Late checkout where possible. Private transfer to El Alto — timed without drama for your international flight.
