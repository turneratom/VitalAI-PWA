The route

Two countries. One unhurried arc.

Trains, transfers, and tables are arranged. You keep the hours that don’t need a plan.

Day 01

Arrive Lisbon

Private transfer from the airport. Settle into a hillside stay with river light. Evening walk and a reserved dinner — nothing after that.

Day 02

Lisbon at walking pace

Morning with a local host through Alfama or the miradouros that fit your brief. Long lunch. Afternoon free for a tram, a gallery, or the waterfront.

Day 03

Belém or open day

Pastel and tower light if you want movement — or a blank day for a beach run to Cascais, shopping, or sleep. Your call by the night before.

Day 04

Train to Porto

Morning rail north along the coast. Check into a riverside stay. Afternoon at Douro pace; dinner nearby with a host who knows the quieter tables.

Day 05

Porto lanes & cellars

Guided morning through Ribeira and the steep streets timed to miss the crush. Afternoon tasting across the river, or free if you want the bridge walk alone.

Day 06

Douro day or rest

Private drive upriver for vineyard lunch and slow water — or leave the day blank in the city. We hold both options until you choose.

Day 07

South to Seville

Morning transfer and flight or long rail south. Settle into a patio stay in the old quarter. Soft evening: orange trees, a reserved tapas table, early night.

Day 08

Seville at walking pace

Host morning through Santa Cruz and the cathedral quarter without the rush. Long lunch. Afternoon free for a garden, a siesta, or the river.

Day 09

Córdoba day

Private drive to Córdoba for the Mezquita and a lunch that doesn’t need commentary. Return by late afternoon with the light still soft.

Day 10

Andalusian open day

Left blank on purpose — flamenco if you want it, a day trip toward Ronda, or nothing at all in the shade of a courtyard.

Day 11

Slow Seville

One last reserved lunch. Afternoon for Triana, a final mirador, or packing without haste. Early evening free.

Day 12

Departure

Late checkout where possible. Private transfer to Seville airport — timed without drama.
