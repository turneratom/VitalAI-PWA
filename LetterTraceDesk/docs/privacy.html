Privacy — Letter Trace Desk

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Letter Trace Desk is a practice-helper iOS app for children ages 3–5. It does not collect personal data, does not create child accounts, and does not upload letter traces, photos, or audio.

On-device preferences (fence acknowledgment, sitting length, letter pool, speech toggle, unlock cache) stay on the device via Apple AppStorage / UserDefaults. The developer does not run an app server for this product.

In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. This app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track users.

The parental math gate is an adult check for settings and purchase. It is not used to collect information about a child.

Support contact is the email / URL configured in App Store Connect for this app. This stub does not list a personal name or mailing address.
