# Honest fences — Letter Trace Desk

Age band: **3–5** (Kids Category: 5 and under)  
Role: **practice helper**, not a curriculum

## Product fences

- Finger tracing of uppercase letters on a short sitting. Sessions end. No infinite play loop.
- Parent math gate for **settings** and **In-App Purchase**. Purchases are not presented as a kid-facing storefront.
- **No child accounts**, no child-typed identity, no photos, no handwriting upload, no chat, no social, no open web in kid mode.
- **Ad-free.** No third-party analytics SDKs. No tracking (no ATT prompt).
- **Offline:** free letters A–F work without a network. StoreKit needs a network only to fetch or complete the one-time unlock.
- Storage is **on-device preferences only**. App Store privacy nutrition: **Data Not Collected**.
- Speech is on-device and optional (parent toggle).

## What this is not

- Not a replacement for school, a teacher, an occupational therapist, or a handwriting curriculum.
- Not an assessment, grade, diagnosis, or progress report about a child.
- Not a subscription, tip jar, or membership.
- Not medical, therapy, or clinical advice.

## Payments (honest)

- Version 1 IAP is a **one-time** non-consumable unlock for letters **G–Z**.
- Suggested App Store Connect price band: **USD $3.99–$4.99** (storefronts vary; the app shows Apple’s live price).
- **Family Sharing** should be enabled on the IAP in App Store Connect.
- No invented revenue, conversion rates, or “parents spend X” claims in listing copy.

## COPPA / children’s privacy

This app is directed to children. It is designed **not to collect personal information from children**. The parental gate is an App Store Kids Category adult check. It is **not** verifiable parental consent to collect data, because **no child personal data is collected**.

If a future version ever collected child personal information, it would need a hosted privacy policy update, App Store privacy nutrition changes, and a real consent mechanism. Do not add that without a legal review.
