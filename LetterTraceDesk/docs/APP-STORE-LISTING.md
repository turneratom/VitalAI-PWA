## Description

Letter Trace Desk is a calm, finger-friendly way for children ages 3–5 to practice tracing uppercase letters.

A sitting is short on purpose. Choose a few letters, follow the dotted path with a finger, hear the letter spoken, then you’re done. There is no endless loop, no ads, and no chat.

This is a practice helper, not a curriculum, school test, or handwriting diagnosis.

WHAT’S INCLUDED
• Uppercase letter paths with a forgiving dotted guide
• Soft celebration and an optional spoken letter name (on this device)
• Parent math gate for settings
• Ad-free. No child accounts. No tracking.

FREE AND UNLOCK
• Free: letters A–F, including offline use
• Optional one-time purchase: Full Alphabet Unlock (G–Z)
• Not a subscription
• Family Sharing can apply when you enable it for the purchase on your Apple ID / family

PARENT CONTROLS
• Sitting length (3–8 letters)
• Which letters appear in a sitting
• Spoken letter on/off
• Purchases stay behind the grown-up math check

PRIVACY
• Data Not Collected
• Preferences stay on the device
• We do not ask for a child’s name or upload handwriting

Letter Trace Desk is designed for the App Store Kids Category (5 and under). A parent or caregiver should sit nearby for ages 3–5.
