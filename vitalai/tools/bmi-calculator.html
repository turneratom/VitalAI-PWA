BMI Calculator FAQ

What is a healthy BMI?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered normal for adults. Below 18.5 is underweight; 25–29.9 is overweight; 30+ is obese.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For imperial: multiply by 703 after using pounds and inches.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat. Very muscular people may show "overweight" despite low body fat. Consider body composition tests for athletes.
