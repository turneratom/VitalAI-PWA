TDEE FAQ

What is TDEE?

TDEE is the total calories you burn in a day including exercise and daily movement. It equals your BMR multiplied by an activity factor.

How many calories to lose 1 lb per week?

One pound of fat is roughly 3,500 calories. A 500 calorie daily deficit equals about 1 lb lost per week.

Which activity level should I pick?

Be honest — most people overestimate. If you train 3 days/week and sit otherwise, "Moderate" is usually correct.
